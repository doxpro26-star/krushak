Krushak — Crop Damage Relief Scheme

Enterprise platform connecting farmers directly to District Agriculture Office. No middleman, transparent tracking.

Register for Scheme — It's Free

Who can apply?

  • Any farmer with land in Maharashtra (all districts) whose crop damaged due to natural or pollution reasons
  • Damage > 33% area eligible for compensation survey

Documents needed

  • 7/12, Survey number, Crop type & farm size
  • 1 clear photo of damaged field (mobile click is okay)
  • Mobile number for SMS updates

Compensation flow

  1. Farmer submits report → Status Pending
  2. Admin verifies field details/photos → Verified
  3. District officer approves → Approved → DBT to bank account (linked to Aadhaar)
  4. If data incomplete → Rejected with remark, farmer can re-apply
